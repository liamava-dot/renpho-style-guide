RENPHO Style Guide — Packaging

Writing Principles

  • Show, Don't Tell — Use product design images and specs, not emotional slogans
  • USP First — Highlight unique selling point features, not abstract benefits
  • Concise & Precise — Remove storytelling/emotion-driven content, focus on features
  • Consistency — Terminology, format, capitalization unified throughout

Front of Box

ElementDescription
Marketing NameFrom product design team, trademarked, for brand TOMA
Product NameFrom PM, for SKU certification before marketing name finalized
USP Summary5 words max — instant summary of stand-out features

Symbol rule: Front uses &, not and

USP Summary Examples

Do Silent Ultrasonic Motor (Eyeris Zen)
Do Customizable Warmth & Comfort (Solheel Slim Thermal)
Do 50+ Body Metrics, 8 Electrodes, LED Display Readings
Don't Relaxation for Your Eyes (too abstract)
Don't The Scale for Modern Travelers (benefit-focused)

Back of Box

ElementDescription
Short DescriptionExpand key features; remove emotion/storytelling; add light benefit if too short
Icons Quick ReferenceFrom Sales Sheet; must be generic enough to reuse across products
USP ModulesPlug-and-play from Sales Sheet; distill into short clear title

Symbol rule: Back uses and, not &

Short Description Examples

Do Focused mist and adjustable temple massage to enhance comfort (Eyeris Zen)
Do Smart-app connectivity with dial control for creating and storing 8 offline user profiles on the scale directly. (Elis 3X)
Don't Refresh your eyes with a massage designed to target your acupoints with a gentle combination of soothing massage... (too story-like)

Icons Quick Reference

Don't Amplitude Dial (Active Evo) — too product-specific
