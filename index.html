Writing Principles

  • Show, Don't Tell — Use product design images and specs, not emotional slogans
  • USP First — Highlight unique selling point features, not abstract benefits
  • Concise & Precise — Remove storytelling/emotion-driven content, focus on features
  • Consistency — Terminology, format, capitalization unified throughout

Scope

Packaging (color box front/back/side) • User manuals • Amazon listings • Sales Sheets

Grammar & Style

Voice & Person

  • Instructions: imperative mood (tell user what to do directly)
  • Feature intros: The XX function allows/enables…
  • Use active voice and second person (you/your)

DON'T Knee, shoulder and elbow

Scale

Bluetooth Connectivity

Access the RENPHO Health App on your phone to sync your readings. Connect to the scale and chart your body health in daily, weekly, monthly or yearly increments.

Wi-Fi Connectivity

Saves data within the scale and automatically syncs to the user's RENPHO Health App on next use, provided an internet connection is available. For those who seek to track their health daily without needing to sync up with the RENPHO Health App on each use! Simply connect the scale to Wi-Fi to send all unpaired scale readings in one sync* upon opening the app next time.

*Shared Wi-Fi and Bluetooth signal between phone and scale required
